    How OVERHANG pays

    How it pays. Your puck stops at aim × wax, where wax is one random draw (log-normal, σ = 0.10) made before you let go. Below the 90% line: nothing. In the pay zone: 1.25×. Nose over the edge: the multiplier is how far it hangs, from about 1.3× to 10× (L), 14× (M) or 21× (S). Past the edge: it falls.

    Every pull is priced at 96.5%. Shallow and deep pulls short or bust more often, so everything they win is scaled up by a factor that depends only on your aim (“ALL PAYS ×1.13”), and every pull returns the same 96.5% on average before rounding (multipliers are rounded down to 0.01×, so the long-run figure is about 96.3%). The table below is that pricing for your current puck.

    The outcome is decided by the draw and your own aim. Nothing adjusts it afterward: no balance-aware weighting, no streak logic, no near-miss injection. The pricing factor is a function of aim only, shown before release.
